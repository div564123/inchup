Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can cancel at any time:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  • Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Select InchUp and tap Cancel Subscription.

To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or free trial). You keep premium access until the end of the period you've already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstalled InchUp or switched to a new iPhone:

  • Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
  • Open InchUp and go to the subscription screen (or Settings in the app).
  • Tap Restore Purchases.

If your subscription still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase — never send your password or payment details.

How do I request a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I get the most accurate scan?
  • Stand sideways to the camera, in a relaxed natural stance.
  • Keep the phone level (the app shows an indicator) and at about hip height.
  • Use good lighting and wear fitted clothing so your silhouette is visible.
How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and the scans, history and settings stored on your device. You can also email us to request deletion. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is InchUp a medical app?

No. InchUp provides informational posture estimates and wellness exercises. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you feel pain or have a medical condition, consult a healthcare professional before starting any exercise program.
